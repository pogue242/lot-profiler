      Quick guide

      1. Pick a tool. The arrow (pointer) shows any lot's record without changing your selections. Lot and Block select tax lots and blocks; NTAs and Tracts select 2020 neighborhoods and census tracts.
        ➤LotBlockNTAsTracts
      2. Draw, a radius, or measure. Draw traces a boundary (click corners, then the first corner or Enter). Radius measures from a point, an entrance or a station; clicks snap to stations. The ▾ sets which lots count. Measure gives a distance or an area (double-click or Enter to finish).
      3. Compare. Turn on ＋ Add (or hold Shift) to keep earlier selections, then switch between Combined, Side by side and each selection. Merge… joins circles, walksheds or drawn areas into one area; Split separates them again.
      4. Read the profile. Land use, housing by units per building, zoning capacity, building age and zoning districts for whatever is selected. Click a table heading to sort; click a row to leave it out of that chart (the rest recalculate). Click a section's title to fold it, or drag its ⋮⋮ to reorder. Customize charts switches pies and bars, groups units per building and leaves land uses out of every figure.
      5. Change the map. The Map layers sidebar colors lots by land use, units, year built or FAR, with a choice of color schemes (click a legend square to pick your own color). Lots appear from zoom sets where lots replace tax blocks, and Blocks show how each block sums up its lots. Turn on the zoning map, subway, PATH, LIRR and Metro-North there too; the ‹ hides the sidebar.
      6. Share or save. Copy link saves everything on screen in the address. Save map image (bottom right of the map) saves the map with its legend, and each section's PNG saves a chart. Ctrl+Z undoes.

      The history bar at the top switches between PLUTO releases and compares two. About & methods explains every figure and lists the sources.
