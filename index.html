      Quick guide

      1. Pick a tool. The arrow (pointer) shows any lot's record without changing your selections. Lot and Block select tax lots and blocks; NTAs and Tracts select 2020 neighborhoods and census tracts.
        ➤LotBlockNTAsTracts
      2. Draw an area or a radius. Draw traces a boundary (click corners, then the first corner or Enter). Radius measures from a point, an entrance or a station; clicks snap to stations. The ▾ sets which lots count: Intersecting, Centroid within, Majority within or Fully within.
      3. Compare. Turn on ＋ Add (or hold Shift) to keep earlier selections, then switch between Combined, Side by side and each selection. Merge… joins circles, walksheds or drawn areas into one area; Split separates them again.
      4. Read the profile. Land use, housing by units per building, zoning capacity, subway access, building age and zoning districts for whatever is selected. Drag a section's ⋮⋮ to reorder; Customize switches bars and pies, groups units per building and leaves out land uses. Click a section's title to hide it, and a table heading to sort by it. Each section's ⓘ explains how it's calculated.
      5. Change the map. Map layers colors lots by land use, units, zoning, year built or FAR, and switches on the zoning map and subway layers. Zoomed out, each block shows its most common value; lots appear as you zoom in.
      6. Share or export. Copy link saves everything on screen in the address. Download CSV exports the figures, and each section's PNG button saves a chart. Ctrl+Z undoes.

      The history bar at the top switches between PLUTO releases and compares two. About & methods explains every figure and lists the sources.
